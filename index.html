        The integer program, for every setting

        What the programs look like today and in the design, mean hours by group of states

        Designs of the integer program (Online Resource 5 of the article): the smallest degree that keeps whole courses and meets, at once, the general education count, the accreditor’s terms and the program’s choice. Kept mathematics, science and economics courses count toward general education; breadth courses, electives and orientation give way to the count. The minimum is a lower bound: it assumes that any general education course can fill the count, that a title naming an outcome teaches it, and that prerequisites and schedules allow the design.

        Share of the modeled programs whose design (ACCE floors or ABET ANSAC text, binding count, with the overlap) keeps a course of each family whose title names the outcome; at right, the share whose course titles name it at all. An outcome no title names is taken as taught inside other courses and constrains nothing, so a review would ask more of a design than the model does (Online Resource 6 of the article).
