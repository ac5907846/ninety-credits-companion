Ninety credits fit only where general education gives way

General education the program teaches anyway

Precalculus or statistics, physics or geology, macroeconomics, composition and speech are required by the program and stand on the university’s general education list, so they count once toward both. The business core does not: no state university list carries accounting or business law, and only the University of Florida lists principles of management.

The floor if the state counted more of the major

Accreditor floors in full, courses as published, 36 credits of general education over Florida’s area minimums. Business foundations count: accounting, business law and principles of management count toward the 36 (economics already does). Any 12 major credits: Louisiana’s rule for its 90-hour degree.
